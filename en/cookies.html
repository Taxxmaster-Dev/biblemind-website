Cookie information

Which cookies and similar technologies the BibleMind website uses, and how you can manage them.

Effective from 7 October 2026

What we do not use

The current version of biblemind.eu does not use advertising or marketing cookies, or cookies that analyse user behaviour. It does not use Google Analytics, the Meta Pixel, the TikTok Pixel or any similar tracking technology.

Strictly necessary technologies

The website may use technical cookies or similar local storage that are strictly necessary for secure and proper operation. Their purpose may be, for example, to remember the language you selected, to keep the session secure, to manage load or to filter out malicious requests. No separate consent is required for such technologies, because without them the requested service would not work, or would not work securely.

Your language choice is stored in the browser's local storage so that the page opens in the right language on your next visit. The launch notification form sets no cookie of its own and uses no external captcha service.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can delete or block cookies in your browser. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may, however, cause some features of the website to malfunction.

Future changes

If the website introduces analytics, marketing or other non-essential cookies in the future, these may only be activated after the visitor has made a prior choice. In that case this notice and the cookie settings interface will be updated before the new technology is put into use.
